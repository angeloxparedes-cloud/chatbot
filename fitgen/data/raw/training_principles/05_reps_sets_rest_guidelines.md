# Sets, Reps, and Rest Period Guidelines by Goal

## Strength (maximal force production)
- Reps: 1–6 per set
- Sets: 3–6 per exercise
- Rest: 2–5 minutes between sets (full neuromuscular recovery)
- Intensity: 80–95% of one-rep max (1RM)
- Best exercises: barbell compounds (squat, bench, deadlift, overhead press)

## Hypertrophy (muscle growth)
- Reps: 6–12 per set (8–20 also effective if taken close to failure)
- Sets: 3–5 per exercise, 10–20 total sets per muscle group per week
- Rest: 60–90 seconds between sets
- Intensity: 65–85% of 1RM, or RPE 7–9 (leaving 1–3 reps in reserve)
- Best exercises: mix of compound and isolation movements

## Muscular Endurance / General Fitness
- Reps: 12–20+ per set
- Sets: 2–3 per exercise
- Rest: 30–60 seconds between sets
- Intensity: 50–65% of 1RM
- Best exercises: circuit-style compound + isolation combinations

## Fat Loss / Conditioning Emphasis
- Reps: 10–15 per set, often in circuit or superset format
- Sets: 3–4 per circuit, minimal rest between exercises (30–45 seconds),
  longer rest between full circuit rounds (60–90 seconds)
- Combine resistance training with short cardio intervals for calorie
  expenditure; resistance training first in the session to preserve strength
  performance while fresh.

## Translating Goals into Weekly Volume
- Beginners: 8–10 total sets per muscle group per week is sufficient to
  drive adaptation without excessive fatigue.
- Intermediate: 10–16 sets per muscle group per week.
- Advanced: 16–22+ sets per muscle group per week, carefully monitored for
  recovery capacity.

## Rest Between Sessions
- Allow at least 48 hours before training the same muscle group again at
  high intensity.
- Full-body programs (3x/week) naturally provide this; split programs must
  be sequenced so opposing/non-overlapping muscle groups are trained on
  back-to-back days if needed.
